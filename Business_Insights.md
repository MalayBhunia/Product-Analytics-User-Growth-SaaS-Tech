# SQL Business Insights
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

## Q1. Monthly New User Signups
### Business Insight
User acquisition remained relatively consistent across the analysis period, with monthly signups ranging from 1,563
to 1,940 users.The highest monthly signup volume occurred in November 2025,while the lowest occurred in February 2023.

## Q2. User Acquisition by Channel
### Business Insight
Organic Search acquired the highest number of users with 25,107 signups,followed by Google Ads with 20,114 users. LinkedIn contributed the lowest number of users with 7,976 signups, while the remaining channels generated between 9,955 and 14,926 users.

## Q3. User Distribution by Country
### Business Insight
India is the largest user market with 42,022 users, accounting for approximately 42% of the total user base. The USA is the second-largest market with 17,973 users, while Singapore has the smallest user base with 4,960 users.

## Q4. Paid Conversion by Acquisition Channel
### Business Insighs
Instagram recorded the highest paid conversion rate at 63.48%, while Organic Search recorded the lowest at 62.21%. However, the difference between the channels is relatively small, indicating similar paid conversion performance across acquisition sources.

## Q5. Average Sessions per User
### Business Insight
The platform recorded 7,512,229 sessions across 100,000 users, resulting in an average of 75.12 sessions per user during the analysis period.

## Q6. Product Engagement by Subscription Plan
### Business Insight
Free-plan users generated the highest session volume with 5.26 million sessions, mainly due to their larger user base. However, average session duration and pages viewed per session were very similar across all plans, with session duration ranging from 16.15 to 16.23 minutes and pages per session ranging from 3.41 to 3.43.

## Q7. Product Feature Usage
### Business Insight
Authentication generated the highest number of product events with 7.51 million events, followed by Dashboard with 3.74 million. Integrations and Collaboration recorded the lowest usage, with approximately 204K events each.

## Q8. Engagement Level and Paid Conversion
### Business Insight
Highly engaged users showed the highest paid conversion rate at 43.51%, compared with 40.20% for medium-engagement users and 34.54% for low-engagement users. This indicates that stronger product engagement is associated with higher paid conversion.

## Q9. Product Funnel Overview
### Business Insighs
All 100,000 users recorded a login event, while 98,044 reached the dashboard, 78,015 created projects, and 63,251 reached the collaboration stage.

## Q10. Funnel Drop-off Analysis
### Business Insight
The largest funnel drop-off occurred between Dashboard and Project Creation at 20.43%, followed by Project Creation to Collaboration at 18.92%. The Login-to-Dashboard drop-off was relatively low at 1.96%.

## Q11. Paid Conversion by Acquisition Channel
### Business Insight
LinkedIn recorded the highest paid conversion rate at 39.54%, closely followed by Instagram at 39.49%. Organic Search recorded the lowest rate at 38.78%, while the overall difference across channels remained relatively small.

## Q12. Subscription Plan Distribution
### Business Insight
Free is the most widely held initial plan with 70,551 users and 107,183 subscription records. Basic follows with 27,791 users, while Pro and Enterprise have 11,728 and 2,438 users respectively.


## Q13. Subscription Plan Transitions
### Business Insight
The most common plan transition was Free to Basic with 7,818 transitions, followed by Basic to Pro with 2,266. Upgrades were more frequent than downgrades, while Enterprise to Pro recorded 44 transitions.

## Q14. Monthly Revenue Trend
### Business Insight
Monthly revenue increased substantially over the analysis period, rising from $19.05K in January 2022 to approximately $313.78K in August 2026. Revenue reached its highest monthly level of approximately $317.29K in January 2026, indicating strong long-term revenue growth.

## Q15. Revenue by Subscription Plan
### Business Insight
Pro generated the highest total revenue at approximately $5.62 million, followed by Basic at $4.93 million and Enterprise at $3.72 million. Free generated no revenue because it is a zero-priced plan.

## Q16. Top Customers by Revenue
### Business Insight
The highest-revenue customer generated approximately $5.85K, while the second-highest generated approximately $5.81K. The results show that a small group of customers contributes several thousand dollars each in recorded revenue.

## Q17. Overall Customer Churn Rate
### Business Insight
The overall customer churn rate is 9.98%, with 9,984 of the 100,000 users classified as churned.

## Q18. Churn by Subscription Plan
### Business Insight
Free-plan users recorded the highest churn rate at 10.06%, while Pro users recorded the lowest at 8.44%. Basic and Enterprise users recorded churn rates of 8.64% and 8.82% respectively.

## Q19. Customer Retention by Acquisition Channel
### Business Insight
Retention rates were relatively consistent across acquisition channels, ranging from 89.72% for Organic Search to 90.32% for Instagram. The overall difference between channels was less than one percentage point.

## Q20. Product Engagement vs Customer Churn
### Business Insight
Active users showed the highest engagement, with an average session duration of 17.10 minutes and 3.58 pages per session. Churned users averaged 10.31 minutes and 2.39 pages per session, while inactive users recorded the lowest engagement at 9.73 minutes and 2.30 pages per session. This shows a clear association between lower product engagement and churned or inactive account status.

## Q21. Support Activity and Churn
### Business Insight
Users with 3+ support tickets had the highest churn rate at 34.64%, compared with 7.65% among users with no support tickets. Churn also increased from 13.52% for users with one ticket to 22.45% for users with two tickets, indicating a strong association between higher support activity and churn.

## Q22. Marketing Channel Performance
### Business Insight
Referral recorded the highest CTR at 19.96% and the highest conversion rate at 17.19%. Email was the second-highest performer on both metrics, while Instagram recorded the lowest CTR at 2.75%.

## Q23. Marketing Acquisition Cost
### Business Insight
Email recorded the lowest acquisition cost at $0.28 per recorded conversion, followed by Referral at $1.05 and Organic Search at $1.19. LinkedIn had the highest acquisition cost at $28.03 per recorded conversion.

## Q24. Support Ticket Categories and Customer Outcomes
### Business Insight
Technical Issue was the most common support category with 9,981 tickets and also had the highest number of churned users at 1,614. Login / Access was the second-largest category with 6,484 tickets and 1,171 churned users.
